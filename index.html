FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does FitFlow give me both workout and diet plans? +

Yes. You get personalized AI workout plans and nutrition guidance — calorie and macro targets plus meal ideas — together in one app.

What happens if I miss a few days? +

Nothing breaks. FitFlow re-plans your next several days around what you missed and eases you back in — no reset streak, no guilt, no starting over.

Is FitFlow good for beginners? +

Yes. The AI starts you with a realistic full-body or upper/lower plan and scales difficulty based on your actual performance and recovery.

How is FitFlow different from other fitness apps? +

Most apps only track the past. FitFlow gives you an AI coach that sees your full workout and nutrition history and actually changes the plan when life interferes.

Can I use FitFlow if I only track workouts or only track food? +

Absolutely. You can start with just workouts or just nutrition and add the other layer later. The coach works with whatever data you give it.
